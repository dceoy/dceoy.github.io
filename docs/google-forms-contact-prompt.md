# Google Forms contact form prompt

This version omits identifying details about the form owner and preserves the form's
title, description, and question structure.

```text
Create a simple English-language contact form for a personal website.

Title: Contact me
Description: Please fill this form.

Add exactly these questions in this order:
1. Full name — short answer, required.
2. Subject — short answer, optional.
3. Message — paragraph, required.

Keep the form concise and suitable for general professional inquiries. Do not add
questions beyond these three, collect email addresses automatically, or request email
addresses, phone numbers, or other additional contact details. Allow anyone with the
form link to respond without requiring Google sign-in.
```
